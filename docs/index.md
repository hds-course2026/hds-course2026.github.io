## Course purpose

High-dimensional statistics studies problems where the ambient dimension is comparable to—or much larger than—the available sample size. The course emphasizes the **theoretical tools and proof techniques** that let us reason rigorously in this regime, with an eye toward modern statistics and machine learning.

The flow of the course is deliberately cumulative: first build concentration tools, then use them in sparse recovery and covariance estimation, learn information-theoretic techniques for lower bounds, and close with uniform convergence and Rademacher-complexity arguments for generalization.
